## About this blog

**Here, understand China** is a multi-lingual in-depth blog for global readers.

In an era where information is highly fragmented, grand narratives and stereotypes are intertwined, we try to go beyond slogans and filters and return to China's long historical context, vivid social reality and ordinary people's real life experience, to present a three-dimensional, concrete, perceptible and knowable China.

Here we explore:

- **History and Memory**: Understanding the background of contemporary China’s thinking is inseparable from looking back at the trauma, transformation and exploration of its modern and ancient history.
- **Reality and Governance**: From the meals of daily life, the details of urban operation to the logic of grassroots governance, observe how society operates with 1.4 billion people.
- **Culture and Perspective**: Break away from black-and-white ideological slogans and record what Chinese people think, feel and want in plain and rational words.

## Multi-language and subscription support

- **Barrier-free reading in 75 languages**: The entire site interface and all articles support **75 languages** and can be freely switched. No matter which country or region you are from, you can read in your native language.
- **Multi-language full-text RSS subscription**: Each language provides an independent full-text RSS 2.0 feed (switch the language in the upper right corner of the website and click the RSS icon to get the subscription address of the corresponding language), supporting all mainstream RSS readers to directly read the complete article.
- **Open Communication**: Welcome to the [Community Forum](https://github.com/orgs/readcn/discussions) to share your thoughts, ask questions, or participate in multilingual discussions.
